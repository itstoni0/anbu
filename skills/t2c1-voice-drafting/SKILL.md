# t2c1 Voice and Drafting

Apply this style guide when you draft text for Tone.
The text may be a chat message, a PR description, a doc, or an email.

## Technical content uses ASD-STE100

Write PR titles and PR descriptions in ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English.
Write commit messages in ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English.
Write Linear issues, GitHub issues, and their comments in ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English.
Write code comments, READMEs, and docs in ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English.
t2c1 Engineering Standards states the STE rules.
For this technical content, the STE rules win over the relaxed tone below.

Do not use humor in technical content.
Do not use an idiom in technical content.
Use short sentences and the active voice in technical content.
The relaxed voice below applies to chat messages and to email.

## Audience first

Confirm the audience before you draft copy.
Give an engineer the technical detail that the engineer needs.
Stay at a high level for a manager or for leadership.
Do not give implementation detail to that audience.

## Tone and format

Keep the text short.
Cut any text that does not change what the reader knows.
Cut any text that does not change what the reader does.
Use relaxed, clear, plain language in chat and in email.
Humor is welcome in chat and in email when the point stays crisp.
Playfulness is welcome in chat and in email when the point stays crisp.

Never use an emoji.
Never use an em dash.
Use a plain dash `-` in place of an em dash.
Use an exclamation point rarely.
Do not use an exclamation point as the default.

## Greetings, sign-offs, and pushback

Open a chat or an email with a short hello.
Use a form such as "Hey X".
Do not skip the hello.
Do not use a formal hello.
Close an email with "Best,".
Then add the email signature for Tone.

Do not type the name for Tone by hand.
When you push back, soften the first line.
Use a question or a short acknowledgment.
Two examples are "Have we considered..." and "I see the reasoning, but...".
Then state the concern in plain text.
Then state the alternative in plain text.
